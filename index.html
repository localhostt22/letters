SOMEWHERE BETWEEN THEN AND NOW

Dear you,

I spent a long time thinking about what I wanted to write here.

And honestly, every version felt .

Because there are some things that are difficult to put into words.

Like how one person can somehow become such a huge part of your life.

How ordinary conversations become memories.

How stupid jokes become things you remember years later.

You noticed.

Four years now. Roughly fourteen hundred and sixty days, and it's still the ordinary ones I'd choose to live again.

I suppose I could say that I love you because you are perfect.
No.

I love you because you are you.

And somewhere along the way, I stopped imagining my future without you in it.

That's probably the simplest way I know how to explain it.

There is something I forgot to tell you.
YOU FOUND THE END

It was never about finding the answer.

It was about finding your way through everything I wanted to say.

Happy Birthday, Saniya.
